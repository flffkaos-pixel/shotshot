## First comment (POST IMMEDIATELY when you launch)

> Hey! I'm [Your Name], I built Shotshot because I got tired of two things:
>
> 1. **Paying $9–$25/month** for screenshot tools when I only launch 2 apps a year
> 2. **Getting "inaccurate metadata" rejections** because my caption text didn't actually match my screenshots
>
> So I built a free, open-source alternative that fixes both.
>
> **Three things make this different:**
>
> 🤖 **OCR caption-verify** — Tesseract.js (client-side, free) checks that the words in your headline actually appear in the screenshot. Prevents App Review rejections. This is the "anti-rejection" feature no one else has.
>
> ✨ **AI captions, but honest** — Bring your own OpenAI key. Your data, your bill, our servers never see the prompt. OpenRouter free models work as fallback. Or use the GUI — no AI lock-in.
>
> 🌏 **80+ locales, one click** — Translate every slide into Korean, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, and 20+ more. Cultural adaptation, not literal translation.
>
> **Pricing:**
> - **Free forever** — 1 project, every size, every locale, every feature
> - **Pro $5/mo** — unlimited projects + cloud sync + AI captions without BYOK
> - **Lifetime $49** — one-time payment, no renewals
>
> **Open source** (MIT) — fork it, self-host it, customize it.
>
> Built with Next.js 15, Supabase, PayPal, R2. Edge runtime on Cloudflare Pages.
>
> Try it: https://shotshot.app
>
> I'd love your feedback — what screenshot tool do you use now, and what's missing? 🙏
>
> — P.S. The 3D mockup I built for v0 was 250kB of Three.js and I ripped it out. Turns out 2D rotation + soft shadow looks better and exports 10x faster. Sometimes less is more.
